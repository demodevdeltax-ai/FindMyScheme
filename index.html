The Challenge

The Government Has Created the Support.
Most Businesses Don't Know It Exists.

There are hundreds of Central and State schemes designed specifically for businesses at every stage — capital investment, hiring, export, manufacturing, technology, women-led enterprises, MSMEs and more. The problem isn't eligibility. It's awareness.

Scattered Across Portals
Central schemes live on one set of ministries. State incentives live on another. Sector-specific programs sit somewhere else entirely. No one has mapped it for your business.
Eligibility is Layered
Whether you qualify depends on your industry, location, business age, turnover, investment level, hiring plans, and what you intend to do next. Generic lists don't account for any of this.
Founders Don't Have Time
Running a business is already a full-time job. Researching government schemes requires hours of reading complex policy documents most founders simply cannot spare.
