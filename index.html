The Problem

You shouldn't need to become a
scheme expert to find support.

Schemes exist across Central and State Governments. But the information is scattered — and piecing it together rarely gives a clear answer to the one question that matters: "Does this actually apply to my business?"

📄Schemes buried in ministry and department PDFs
🗺️State-level benefits that rarely get mentioned
📋Sector-specific funds invisible to generalist advisors
⏰Deadlines that pass before anyone hears of them
🔄Eligibility criteria that change — old versions keep circulating
🌐Dozens of portals, each with its own search logic

// What usually happens

01
Start with a generic search
"Government schemes for my business in India" — a list of results, some of which were discontinued years ago.
→ Unreliable results
02
Ask a CA, consultant, or AI
They may know tax and a couple of MSME schemes. Sector-specific and state-specific opportunities usually aren't their focus.
→ Partial picture at best
03
Browse the portals yourself
Portals across ministries and departments — each with its own logic, with eligibility buried inside long policy documents.
→ Hours lost, still incomplete
04
End up with information, not an answer
A pile of documents but no clarity on what actually applies — so most people move forward without the full picture.
→ Opportunity missed
