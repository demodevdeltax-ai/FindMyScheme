The Problem

The information is out there.
Finding it is the problem.

India has thousands of active government schemes. Founders don't know most of them exist. The ones who claim to know are often guessing.

📄Schemes buried in ministry PDFs no one reads
🗺️State-level benefits that few mention
📋Sector-specific funds invisible to generalist advisors
⏰Deadlines that pass before anyone hears about them
🔄Schemes that changed eligibility — the old version gets shared
🏢Consultants who charge ₹50,000+ just to identify options

01
Start with a generic Google search
"Government schemes for food business India" — gets a list of 15 schemes, 4 of which were discontinued in 2021.
→ Unreliable results
02
Ask a CA or consultant
They know tax. They may know a couple of MSME schemes. Sector-specific, state-specific, or novel schemes? Usually not their focus.
→ Partial picture at best
03
Browse individual ministry portals
40+ portals. Each with its own search logic. No aggregated view. Eligibility criteria scattered across 12-page PDFs.
→ Hours lost, still incomplete
04
Give up or proceed without the full picture
Most founders move forward without knowing what they were eligible for. Some learn later. Most never do.
→ Opportunity missed
